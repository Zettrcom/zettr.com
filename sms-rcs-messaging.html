SMS and RCS Messaging

Amili Pay helps businesses send invoices and collect payments. This page explains when you may get a text message from us, why you get it, and how to stop receiving messages.

What We Send

We only send transactional messages about an invoice you have with a business, for example:

New Invoice

Containing a secure payment link to complete your transaction.

Payment Reminder

A helpful reminder for an upcoming or unpaid invoice.

Receipt & Confirmation

Immediate proof of payment once your invoice is settled.

We never send marketing or promotional messages.

How You Opt In

Online Consent

When you place an order, book a service, or sign up with a business on its website, you enter your mobile number. The form states that the number will be used to send invoices, payment links, and receipts by SMS.

In Person or by Phone

When you buy something in a shop or agree to a service over the phone, the staff ask for your mobile number and tell you the invoice and payment link will be sent by SMS. You can say no and get the invoice by email instead.

Privacy Guarantee: Your number is saved on your invoice in Amili Pay and is only used for messages about that specific invoice.

Standard Reply Commands

  • STOP Unsubscribes your number immediately from future SMS/RCS notifications.
  • START Resubscribes your number if you previously opted out.
  • HELP Provides direct support information for the issuing merchant.

Note: Opting out applies to all automatic invoice notifications sent through the Amili Pay platform sender for that phone number.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP (or STOPP) to any message.

You will immediately receive this confirmation message and no further automated communications will be sent:

"You have been unsubscribed and will not get any more messages from Amili Pay. Reply START to subscribe again. For help, contact the business that sent you the invoice."

You may reply START at any time to re-subscribe, or reply HELP for support instructions.

RCS and SMS Compatibility

Where your phone and mobile carrier support it, messages arrive as rich RCS (Rich Communication Services) messages with verified branding. If RCS is not available, the exact same message is delivered as a standard SMS automatically.

The opt-out footer and the STOP reply function identically on both RCS and standard SMS formats.

Businesses Using Their Own Twilio Account

A business can choose to send messages from its own custom Twilio account instead of the shared Amili Pay platform account.

The same opt-out line is added to every message, but the business is directly responsible for opt-out handling, including RCS opt-out compliance, and must ensure no SMS notifications are sent to customers who have unsubscribed.

* Note: This page specifically describes messages sent through the standard Amili Pay platform sender.
